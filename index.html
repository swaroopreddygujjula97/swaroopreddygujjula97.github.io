The story

Every manufactured part tells you the truth about the system that made it. For eight years I have led quality in high-precision, zero-defect manufacturing — most recently as Quality Assurance Manager at Yorozu Automotive Alabama, directing an 8+ person QA organization across two shifts and four global OEM programs.

I implement the systems (IATF 16949, APQP/PPAP, FMEA), I run the problem-solving (8D, CAPA, SCARs), I manage the supply base — and then I digitalize all of it, because quality you can’t see in real time is quality you don’t have.

This is the record: three programs, three measurable outcomes — and an open door to mission-critical manufacturing, including data-center infrastructure.

02FIG. 02 — WHERE VARIATION HIDES
Chapter two — Variation

Chasing variation to zero.

Scrap was eroding margins on high-volume programs — defects caught late, contained expensively.

THE WORK

Built SPC-driven process controls, layered process audits, and a disciplined 8D corrective-action loop — so variation got caught at the source instead of at final inspection.

THE RESULT

A controlled, capable process, held quarter after quarter.

“Scrap is a story the process is telling you. Learn to read it early and it stops being expensive.”
